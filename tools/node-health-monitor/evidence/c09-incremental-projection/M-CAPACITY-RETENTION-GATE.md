# C09 M evidence capacity and retention gate — RED

This is a read-only capacity audit on 2026-09-30, not a retention implementation, a 72-hour result, or authorization to delete live evidence. The business, Edge, M, and QueryService services were not changed by this audit.

## Observed timeline

- At 2026-09-29 23:24:20–23:28:34 UTC, the live M `evidence.db` main file grew from 44,482,560 to 45,830,144 bytes: 1,347,584 bytes in 254 seconds, or about 18.2 MiB/hour. The independent 88-minute sample in the [M-only quota bridge receipt](/home/tomi/nhm-supervision/c09-local/m-quota-update-20260929T233022Z/M-QUOTA-BRIDGE.md) measured 19.15 MiB/hour. Under the then-configured 256 MiB cap, a 72-hour run could not be expected to finish at that rate.
- At 23:31:37 UTC, the supervisor backed up M's evidence and control SQLite databases, then changed **only** the local M `evidence_quota_bytes` from 268,435,456 to 2,147,483,648. The public manifest records `retention_implemented=false` and `production_accepted=false`. The live manager is active at PID 4,127,758 with no unit restarts, and the private manager config still states 2,147,483,648 bytes. This is a reviewed local capacity bridge, not cleanup.
- A read-only `stat` at 2026-09-30 02:37 UTC found `evidence.db` 107,327,488 bytes, its WAL 531,512 bytes, and its SHM 32,768 bytes. These are separate physical files; SQLite `max_page_count` caps the main database's pages, not total evidence storage, backups, or WAL. At the earlier 19.15 MiB/hour mean, 72 more hours would add about 1.35 GiB to the main file, leaving nominal room below 2 GiB. This is a projection from a short sample, **not** a worst-case A–F/72-hour measurement or a disk-reservation proof.
- The containing filesystem reported 588,851,392,512 bytes available in that read-only check. The projected 72-hour main-file increment is much smaller, but this is shared free space, **not** a reservation for M. The quota bridge's 2-GiB main-file setting and its separate WAL check do not budget all physical WAL, control/Q databases, logs, and consistent backups together; their peak simultaneous footprint and competing use must be measured before a disk-safety claim.

## Why the 72-hour gate remains RED

`EvidenceDb::open` sets SQLite `max_page_count` and checks the WAL file against the configured quota. Its `observations` and `witness_observations` tables append immutable rows; the current M source has no age-based DELETE, bounded compaction, or event/metric retention worker. A quota stops growth with an error; it does not release old rows. The evidence and control databases, WAL, logs, snapshots/backups, and QueryService ledger have separate physical budgets. No accepted 72-hour Q-aware stream or near-full/restore drill proves that the local 2-GiB bridge remains safe under the required profile. Therefore C09's retention/capacity/72-hour gate is **RED/open**, even though the old 256-MiB imminent limit has been mitigated locally.

## Safe closure boundary

1. Freeze a retention policy by evidence class and reference state against R4 §10: initial metrics 14 days, events 7 days, resolved incidents 30 days, evidence 2 GiB; active incidents cannot silently lose required evidence. Define `evidence_expired`/unknown behavior before removing any original M parent. Preserve fixed-W grants, retained QueryService parent `store_seq`/body/hash bindings, global cursor/high-water, source quarantine, and outbox/incident references. Do not reuse store sequences or convert missing evidence into healthy state.
2. Implement bounded, indexed pruning/checkpoint work in isolated copies first, with one writer and rollback-safe transactions. Test active fixed-W refusal, later expiry/release, restart during pruning, parent already archived in Q, quarantine, near-quota write refusal, WAL/checkpoint and consistent online backup/restore. Avoid live `DELETE`/`VACUUM` until the deletion set and dependent references have been independently reviewed.
3. Measure main DB, WAL, control DB, Q ledger, backups, and free disk through a new Q-aware 72-hour run under the approved A–F profile. Report peak and growth with margin, no grant/source laundering, continuity and near-cap response. A nominal 2-GiB setting or `max_page_count` alone cannot pass this gate.
