## Source-fixture truth

- `native-core.json` and `native-core.prom` were generated by the isolated
  production exporter actor with a synthetic collector. They prove the frozen
  contract subset, not a production node or performance gate.
- `process-source.json` is a synthetic fixed-field envelope matching the
  implemented process adapter contract. It deliberately reports partial
  coverage and names missing host/cgroup/FD/core fields.
- Sources without a lawful fixture remain `fixture=null`, disabled and
  `contract_valid=false`; no zero or healthy sample is substituted.
