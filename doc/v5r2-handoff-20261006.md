## Design contract: DESIGN-20261003.md

Source: [design at memo commit 106ed7ee](https://github.com/tosnetwork/memo/blob/106ed7eede080b8bde5288e8fbcde1ce3e2dbc0a/wallet-slhdsa-rescue/DESIGN-20261003.md), blob `9e057d098422981b752ff23b242799e310ab26a9`. The requirements are summarized here so continuation does not depend on access to the memo repository.

### Authorization and scope

- **PRIMARY is ML-DSA-44 from creation. SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s is the independently registered hash-based protection/recovery authority.** No Ed25519 authorization, classical cosigning, mode 3 or classical recovery/payer dependency may be required by the wallet flow. Any legacy-shaped key field is inert and its canonical encoding must be frozen.
- V5R2 retains the full V5R1 action/transaction engine and equally strict send modes, replay protection, counters and authority transitions. Keep V5R1 behavior/identity intact. R2 refuses missing/truncated AUTH state, legacy AUTH/signature/extension entry points, modes 0/1/3 and authority-bypass actions such as SETCODE or self-destruction/redeployment.
- These wallet/module/vault contracts are **basechain/workchain 0 only**, enforced on-chain even for hand-built StateInit. Masterchain validator/stake-controller rescue and Agent Account rescue are outside this wallet design.
- `RESCUE_READY`: eligible, unretired PRIMARY may execute value operations; SLH may execute them too. A primary forger can spend before lock/retirement, but cannot replace rescue authority. Only remaining assets are recoverable.
- `SLH_REQUIRED`: SLH is required for every outgoing value/authority operation. PRIMARY is refused. These policies do **not** mean ML-DSA AND SLH signatures on every transaction.
- PRIMARY may never configure, lock or migrate. SLH may execute, configure, lock and perform the bounded recovery migration. The module and final receiving wallet independently enforce the role/policy/retirement matrix, including a PRIMARY relay delivered after retirement.

### Contracts, wire identity and recovery

- An immutable dual-root module stores the network/global ID, primary suite/key, SLH key and policy. The installed wallet tuple includes code/profile identity, local retired bits, AUTH epoch, independent role nonces and an active fee-route descriptor. Signed canonical witnesses determine the tuple, not relayer assertions.
- AUTH v2 binds domain/version, network/global ID, wallet, installed module, role, epoch, role nonce, expiry, operation and full payload. PRIMARY cannot consume the rescue nonce or exhaust recovery. Control operations advance the epoch and reset role counters with checked, nonwrapping arithmetic; ordinary execute increments only its role nonce.
- Global ConfigParam 48 and local retirement must be monotonic. Retirement cannot be cleared by setters, policy changes or successor installation. Malformed policy fails PRIMARY closed while preserving local SLH rescue where the design permits it.
- Module/vault StateInit validation must bound code/data, ordinary cells, split depth, special flags, libraries, trailing data, network, suite, keys, wallet, target and policy. A fresh successor needs distinct keys, a fresh fee tree and authenticated proof of possession for **both** keys.
- The fee vault's target is immutable. Rotating the wallet's security tuple therefore stages a **new paired module and vault**, then atomically installs the tuple at the **same wallet address**. This is not an asset transfer or a V5R1-to-V5R2 migration.
- SLH-only preparation through the old route may deploy/fund the validated successor pair using incoming funds. It does not move wallet assets or withdraw old module reserves. Partial deployment leaves wallet authority unchanged. Prove the successor fee route/POPs before migration; prove actual recipient execution after it. Nonce consumption, exit 0 or a send action alone is insufficient.

### Crypto, fee state and backup

- Generic `PQCHECKSIG_SUITE` supports suites 1 ML-DSA-44, 2 Falcon-512 padded, 3 SLH-SHA2-128s and 4 the fixed fee verifier. Genesis candidate is version 18; both Falcon entry points must reject at 18 and work at 19. Other introduction minima need not all equal the genesis version.
- SLH profile: 32-byte public key and 7,856-byte signature, canonical ordinary byte chains, exact external context semantics and independent fixtures/interop. Freeze opcode operands, errors, tariffs, TL-B, code hashes and full vectors.
- Fee authorization is one-level HSS/LMS **H20/W4 only**: 60-byte HSS public key, 2,832-byte HSS signature, 23-cell canonical signature chain. Reject bare LMS, alternate heights/widths/types, bad L/Nspk/q/context and extra bytes. Terminal leaf state is `2^20`; never wrap.
- LMS pays only the specified **SLH recovery AUTH, POP and preparation** classes. It gives no wallet asset authority and cannot fund PRIMARY AUTH. Verify signature, class/role/identity, leaf/slot/TTL, input bounds, live tariffs, solvency and amount/reserve bounds **before ACCEPT**.
- Fee state uses one-hour slots and four leaves per slot, bounded deadlines and a next-slot wait after restore. Durable ordering is intent/reservation before signing, verify before cache/export, and exact cached retry with no fresh signer call. Crash, concurrency, stale copies, uncertain writes and device restore must not silently reuse OTS state.
- A mnemonic alone is not an intact LMS journal. The slot/loss model deliberately trades bounded fee exposure for recovery liveness. Fresh-tree replacement and custody/readiness invalidation are mandatory.
- `TOS-WALLET-DUALROOT-KDF-v1` uses RFC 5869 HKDF-SHA256 with distinct ML/SLH labels and network/global ID/account index/key generation in the context. ML seed is 32 bytes; SLH seed is 48 bytes. Fee derivation is separately domain-separated and fresh on replacement. Native TOS 24-word generation/seed derivation is **not BIP39**; raw master import is exactly 32 random bytes. Record all seed/KDF profiles. High-value SLH custody should be independently cold.

## Gas plan: GAS-ADMISSION-SOLUTION-20261006.md

Source: [gas plan at memo commit 106ed7ee](https://github.com/tosnetwork/memo/blob/106ed7eede080b8bde5288e8fbcde1ce3e2dbc0a/wallet-slhdsa-rescue/GAS-ADMISSION-SOLUTION-20261006.md), blob `b69213369c878f55093b6f50306b49ffdd948c42`. Its investigation reviewed older TOS head `91a8f31d…`; it is a diagnosis/proposal, not a declaration that later code or deployment passed.

- Ordinary external `gas_credit=10,000` bounds unpaid execution before acceptance. It is not a flat fee, transaction total gas cap, signature security parameter or TPS quota. The actual initial credit also depends on available balance/gas limits.
- Recorded complete fee paths at the older diagnostic profile require AUTH **12,600**, POP **13,190**, PREP **13,515** gas; fixed LMS work is **6,135** (`500 + 3 × 1,067` plus cell/instruction costs). Thus the old credit blocks intact requests before ACCEPT.
- Candidate solution: **basechain ConfigParam 21 credit 20,000**, masterchain ConfigParam 20 credit **10,000**, basechain gas limit **30,000,000**, block gas limit **60,000,000**; keep the fixed LMS profile and current crypto tariffs/guards. Candidate acceptance margin is selected complete legal paths **≤18,000 gas including ACCEPT**, leaving ≥2,000. This is a regression target, not a formal universal worst-case proof.
- Couple the configuration change with shared all-source admission-work charging, bounded input/queues/state and removal of default failed-VM diagnostic replay. Credit 20,000 applies to all relevant ordinary basechain external contracts, so a vault-only benchmark is insufficient.
- Work profiles must price supported initial execution, special/masterchain/native destinations, parse/lookup/result cost, cold state, inflight bursts and fairness. Never turn warm median microbenchmarks or actor count into a production rate. Unsupported profiles fail closed; local rejection must not change block consensus validity.
- **Important later verified clarification:** the real node checker sets `stop_on_accept_message=true`; ACCEPT and SETGASLIMIT take the stop path. The owner-controlled probe uses **436 gas in the checker**, but **116,106 / 30,000,000** in full transaction execution. Full post-ACCEPT gas is not the ordinary checker VM bound. The initial gas helper and full-transaction helper serve different purposes. Neither is a CPU quote or a calibrated deployment rate.
